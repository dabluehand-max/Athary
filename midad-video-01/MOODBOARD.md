# MOODBOARD: مِداد (Midad) Motion Direction

Status: Building 3 visual directions from collage + cut-out animation reference

## Reference Research Sources
- YouTube: collage animation, cut-out animation, paper stop-motion
- Historical: Hannah Höch (Dada), Terry Gilliam (cut-out), contemporary artists

### Direction 1: Warm Textile Collage (Primary)
**Concept**: Handmade cut-paper figures stitched into motion, textile-forward
- **Palette**: Cream (#EFE6D6), Deep Navy (#0B1A3A), Sadu reds, Mustard sun, thin red thread
- **Texture**: Flat cut-outs with soft shadows, slight paper grain visible
- **Motion Timing**: Slow, deliberate, 12 fps jitter within 30 fps (handmade feel)
- **Type Treatment**: Amiri serif for headers (classical Arabic), IBM Plex Sans Arabic for body (clean)
- **Reference**: Brand identity existing in logos — already collage-ready

### Direction 2: High-Contrast Ink Stain (Experimental)
**Concept**: Blue pigment smearing, organic ink-to-paper, visceral
- **Palette**: Navy, cream, raw indigo, white negative space
- **Texture**: Wet media, sharp edges, some bleeding
- **Motion**: Faster transitions, more aggressive cuts
- **Reference**: Workshop footage of blue figure canvas (d5189b1a-93e6… and cc00a9b5…)

### Direction 3: Layered Figure Drawing (Illustrative)
**Concept**: Pencil line + collage overlay, continuous drawing motion
- **Palette**: Warm ochre + navy + creative mark-making colors
- **Texture**: Sketch lines visible, layered transparencies
- **Motion**: Reveal via drawing, then cut-out movement
- **Reference**: Workshop scenes with inking (1cafbd0f…) and large canvas (76244a81…)
